## Acme Consulting's Remote Work Policy Summary

Based on the Retrieval Agent's findings from **remote-work-policy.pdf**, here are the key provisions:

**Eligibility**
Employees must complete a 90-day probationary period and must not require regular physical presence at client sites. New hires documented as fully remote in their offer letter are exempt from the waiting period. The policy covers full-time and part-time employees only — not independent contractors.

**Availability**
Remote employees must be available between **10:00 AM and 3:00 PM in their local time zone**. Schedules are otherwise flexible, provided weekly hour expectations and client meetings are met.

**Equipment & Security**
The company provides a laptop, monitor, and VPN access. Company data must remain on company-issued devices and cannot be stored on personal cloud storage, email, or removable media. VPN use is mandatory on public Wi-Fi, and lost/stolen equipment must be reported to IT Security **within four hours**.

**Home Office Stipend**
Employees may claim up to **$500 per year** for home office equipment, submitted by **December 15**. Unused amounts do not roll over, and internet/utilities are not covered.

**Performance & Review**
Arrangements are reviewed annually. Managers must provide a **30-day improvement window** before revoking remote work privileges, except in cases of security violations.

**International Work**
Temporary work from another country is permitted up to **20 business days per year**, subject to manager approval and an IT Security review.
